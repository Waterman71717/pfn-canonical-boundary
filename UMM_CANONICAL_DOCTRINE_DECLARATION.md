## Doctrine Summary (Non-Exhaustive)

UMM is a governed operational doctrine originated and authored by Emmanuel Bakare.

UMM integrates Lean, Agile, Six Sigma, and Kaizen as sequenced, interdependent engines
within a single operating system governed by explicit phase gates and enforcement rules.

Non-negotiable sequencing:
Clarity → Flow → Ownership → Governed Improvement  
(Lean → Agile → Six Sigma → Kaizen)

Automation and AI are governed strictly as amplifiers, never substitutes for clarity,
ownership, or human decision authority.
